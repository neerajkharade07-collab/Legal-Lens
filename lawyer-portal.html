How it works

One connected registration that takes a few minutes. When you finish, your dashboard opens and clients can find you; requests open after a basic CASEONE review.

01

Account

Name, email, mobile number and password.

02

Professional profile

City, state, photograph, State Bar Council, enrollment number, practice areas and consultation type: Paid Lawyer, Free Lawyer or Both.

03

Documents

Masked Aadhaar, PAN card, Sanad / enrollment certificate and law degree, stored privately.

04

Dashboard and listing

Your dashboard opens and your profile is listed in the paid directory. Requests — and the Free Legal Aid directory — open after a basic CASEONE review.

05

Manage requests

Accept or decline consultation requests. Paid and free requests are kept separate.

What you need

  • Your State Bar Council, enrollment number and enrollment date
  • Masked Aadhaar (downloaded from UIDAI, only the last 4 digits visible)
  • PAN card
  • Advocate Sanad / Bar Council enrollment certificate
  • Law degree (LL.B.) certificate — each file PDF, JPG or PNG, max 5 MB
  • A professional photograph

How your documents are handled

  • Stored privately — never on your public profile and never in a public folder
  • Opened only by authorised CASEONE reviewers; every view is logged
  • Aadhaar must be masked; a file showing the full number is refused. For Aadhaar and PAN we keep only the last 4 characters
  • Uploading a document does not mark it as verified, and reading a document is never treated as proof that it is genuine. No facial recognition is used
  • You can replace documents while registering, and ask us to delete them afterwards

Paid, Free or Both — one account

Set your paid consultation fee, volunteer for free consultations (always ₹0, only for applicants approved by CASEONE), or offer both. Your profile shows factual information only: no ratings, rankings or promotional claims, in keeping with the Bar Council of India rules on advocates' conduct.
