Getting Started

What is Legal Lens?

Legal Lens is an AI-powered platform designed to help citizens understand complex legal documents in simple language and identify important information such as deadlines, warnings, and possible next steps.

How does Legal Lens work?

Upload a legal document such as a PDF or image. Legal Lens processes the document, extracts relevant information, and presents the key points in an easier-to-understand format.

What types of documents can I upload?

Legal Lens can be designed to support documents such as court notices, FIR-related documents, property papers, tenancy agreements, and loan recovery notices.

Do I need legal knowledge to use Legal Lens?

No. Legal Lens is designed to make complex legal information easier to understand for people without a legal background.

Document Analysis

Can Legal Lens analyze PDF documents?

Yes. The platform is designed to process supported PDF documents and extract relevant text for analysis.

Can I upload an image of a legal document?

Yes. Supported image formats can be processed using OCR technology to extract text from scanned or photographed documents.

What information can Legal Lens identify?

Depending on the document, the system can highlight important dates, deadlines, names, amounts, warnings, key clauses, and suggested next steps.

Can Legal Lens detect fraudulent documents?

Legal Lens can help identify potentially suspicious information or inconsistencies. However, these results should be treated as warnings rather than proof of fraud.

AI & Legal Guidance

Does Legal Lens provide legal advice?

Legal Lens is designed primarily to provide educational information, document explanations, and general guidance. It should not replace advice from a qualified legal professional.

How accurate is the AI?

AI-generated results may contain errors or misunderstand legal context. Important legal decisions should always be verified using official sources or with a qualified legal professional.

Can Legal Lens tell me what I should do next?

It can provide general next-step suggestions based on the information identified in a document. Users should verify these suggestions before taking important legal action.

Privacy & Security

Is my legal document private?

Legal documents can contain highly sensitive information. Legal Lens should use appropriate security measures and privacy practices to protect uploaded information.

Does Legal Lens store my documents?

Document storage depends on the platform's configured data-retention policy. The interface should clearly inform users about how their documents are handled.

Can I use Legal Lens without saving my document?

Where supported, Privacy Mode can allow users to process documents with reduced or temporary data retention.

Lawyer Connect

Can I connect with a lawyer through Legal Lens?

Yes. Legal Lens can provide a Lawyer Connect feature that helps users find or connect with qualified legal professionals when additional assistance is required.

When should I contact a lawyer?

You should consider consulting a qualified lawyer when your situation involves significant legal consequences, deadlines, disputes, court proceedings, or when you are unsure about your legal rights.

e-Courts & Government Portals

Legal Lens can provide links and guidance to relevant official government and e-Courts portals so users can verify information using trusted sources.

The platform can guide users toward official e-Courts services where they can verify case-related information. Official government portals should be treated as the authoritative source.

Legal information can change and AI-generated explanations may contain errors. Official government and court portals provide authoritative information for verification.
