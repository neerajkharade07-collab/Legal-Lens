What you can rely on

CASEONE lists advocates factually. There are no paid placements, rankings or star ratings.

Review status shown

Advocates are listed when they register. Profiles marked “Credentials reviewed by CASEONE” have had a basic check by our team (not official Bar Council verification), and only those accept requests. You can report any profile.

Clear fees

Paid consultation fees are listed on every profile. Free consultations are always ₹0.

Private documents

Documents you upload are stored privately and are visible only to authorised reviewers.

Honest status

A request is never shown as confirmed until the lawyer has actually accepted it.

Looking for government legal aid?

CASEONE free assistance is a volunteer program run on this platform with independent advocates. It is not government legal aid, and CASEONE does not appoint legal-aid advocates. Free legal services under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 are provided by the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and your State, District or Taluka Legal Services Authority.
