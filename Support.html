Uploading a Document

Select a supported file from your device and upload it directly through the document upload area. Supported formats include PDF and common image types.

Document Processing

Once uploaded, your document is processed to extract key information. Processing time depends on document length and image quality.

OCR & Image Quality

For scanned or photographed documents, OCR accuracy improves with clear, well-lit, high-resolution images free of glare or blur.

AI Explanation

Legal Lens breaks down complex legal language into plain explanations, highlighting key dates, clauses, and suggested next steps.

Language & Translation

Explanations can be provided in multiple supported regional languages, with more languages added over time.

Text-to-Speech

Enable voice output to have explanations read aloud in your selected supported language.

Privacy Mode

Privacy Mode allows documents to be processed with reduced or temporary data retention, where supported.

Lawyer Connect

Connect with a qualified legal professional directly through the platform when you need additional assistance.

e-Courts & Government Portals

Access links and guidance toward official government and e-Courts portals to verify information using trusted sources.

Account & Login

Manage your account details, update your password, and troubleshoot sign-in issues from your account settings.

Having trouble?

Try these quick solutions before contacting support.

My document isn't uploading

Check that your file is in a supported format and that your internet connection is stable. Try uploading the document again.

The document analysis is taking too long

Large or image-heavy documents may take longer to process. Wait a moment and check your connection before trying again.

The extracted text is incorrect

Make sure the document image is clear, well-lit, and not blurry. Poor-quality scans may reduce OCR accuracy.

I don't understand the AI explanation

Try asking a more specific question or review the highlighted sections of the document.
