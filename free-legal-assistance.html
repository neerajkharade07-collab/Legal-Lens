Important information · a CASEONE volunteer program, not government legal aid
  • This is a CASEONE volunteer program, not government legal aid. CASEONE does not appoint government legal-aid advocates.
  • Free legal services under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 are provided by NALSA and State, District and Taluka Legal Services Authorities. You can apply there at any time: nalsa.gov.in · helpline 15100. Their eligibility rules may differ from CASEONE's.
  • CASEONE's criterion: annual household income below ₹2,50,000. Every application is reviewed by an authorised CASEONE admin; eligibility is not approved from the income you type.
  • Documents are stored privately. Only you and authorised CASEONE reviewers can open them; they are never shown on lawyer profiles or public pages. CASEONE does not claim official verification of your documents.
  • In an emergency, call 112 (police), 181 (women helpline) or 1098 (Childline).
